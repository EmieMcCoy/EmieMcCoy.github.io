Projects

Analyzing Aggie Buses

GIS analysis of Texas A&M bus system efficiency and route performance

Problem
Texas A&M recently implemented a new centralized bus system with routes designed around Simpson Drill Field. Students complained about inefficient routes and inaccurate bus ETAs, leading to delays and inconveniences.
What I Did
I analyzed seven bus routes by comparing estimated times of arrival from the Aggie Transportation schedule against actual time stamps recorded at each stop. I tracked bus capacity from stop to stop and documented both arrival and departure times to identify problem areas around campus.
What I Found
60%
Buses Off Schedule
40%
Buses On Time

Only about 40% of buses arrived on time according to the Aggie Transportation schedule. Nearly 60% of buses were not where they were supposed to be, with bus route 04 showing only 41% on-time arrivals. Drivers were not accurately tracking bus capacity, creating wait times of 15-20 minutes.

Legend: Green = On Time | Yellow = Late/Left Early | Red = Arrived & Left Before ETA

Research Poster
What I Would Do Differently
I would change the color of the map legend so that it was more accessible for those that are color blind.

Amsterdam Bicycle Infrastructure

GIS analysis of bike lanes and vehicular routes in Amsterdam

Problem
Differentiate the bike and vehicular lanes in Amsterdam
What I Did
I cleaned the dataset and created groups that showed bike-only lanes, bike and moped lanes, bike priority roads, and shared roads with other vehicles.
What I Found
Amsterdam truly prioritizes bikes as most of thier roads are bike friendly. Their infrastructure does not cater to vehicles like the United States does.
What I Would Do Differently
I think I should have clipped the layers a little further out. Instead, I kept just the innermost part of the city.

US Energy Usage

County-level analysis of residential energy consumption by state

Problem

What I Did
I created a map that shows the average amount of energy being used to heat and cool houses in each county.
What I Found
Heating a house uses more energy than air conditioning.
What I Would Do Differently
I would probably change the color scheme of some of the maps.
